Before you start

How to report a problem

In the app go to More, then Report a problem, then Copy report. Paste it into your message and add:

What I did:
What I expected:
What happened:

The report has your app version, phone type and recent errors. It does not include photos, piece names or your city. Screenshots help a lot.

1

First-time setup

You should see
5 short steps, and your city's temperature at the top of Today.

Tell me if
anything was confusing, the city wasn't found, or it got stuck.

2

Take photos

You should see
A dashed guide frame, your photos appearing in the list, and a warning with Undo for the dark photo.

Tell me if
the camera won't open, the screen is black, photos go missing, or warnings were wrong.

3

Smart photos

You should see
Android: backgrounds removed and names like "Navy hoodie". iPhone: plain backgrounds removed and colors detected. Type recognition is off by default on iPhone.

Tell me if
it's stuck for more than 3 minutes, the page reloads or crashes, or the names and colors are often wrong. Copy the report right away.

4

Today's outfit

You should see
A labeled outfit board, a style tip, and an image you can share.

Tell me if
outfits clash, pieces repeat too much, or the tips don't make sense.

5

Log what you wore

You should see
Both days under "Last two weeks", each with an Edit button.

6

Week and laundry

You should see
Seven planned days that respect your laundry day, and counts that match what you logged.

Tell me if
the numbers look wrong.

7

Packing list

You should see
Pieces grouped by type that suit the weather.

8

Backup

You should see
A file saved or the share sheet opening.

Tell me if
nothing downloads or it says the storage is full.
